Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about joining Bioneer Team

What is Bioneer Team?

+

Bioneer Team is a student team specialized in biomedical engineering at Tanta University. We aim to develop our members' skills through workshops, practical projects, and activities that connect theory with practical application in biomedical engineering.

Who can join Bioneer Team?

+

We welcome all Tanta University students who are passionate about biomedical engineering, innovation, and teamwork. While our focus is on engineering students, we also accept members from other faculties who are interested in our tracks.

What tracks are available?

+

We offer four main tracks: Operating Room (technical track), Human Resources (member development), Public Relations (external relations), and Media (content creation and social media). Each track has specific responsibilities and training programs.

How often does the team meet?

+

We have weekly general meetings and track-specific sessions. Additionally, we organize workshops, training sessions, and project meetings throughout the semester. The schedule is flexible to accommodate academic commitments.

Is there a membership fee?

+

Yes, there is a small annual membership fee that covers workshop materials, team activities, and administrative costs. The exact amount is announced during recruitment and varies based on the track.
